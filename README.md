## 🧠 About Me

Hey, I'm Sakshi — an AI Consultant at Heartland Community Network, where I spend my days building and shipping LLM-powered and agentic systems that actually run in production, not just in a notebook.

I hold an MS in Data Science from Indiana University (GPA 3.9/4.0) and an MS in Applied Statistics, and what I love most is the full arc of a project: designing an agent pipeline, training and evaluating a model, then wiring up the infrastructure — APIs, containers, monitoring — so it survives contact with the real world. Lately that's meant a lot of time in LangGraph, MLflow, and Kubernetes.

I'm based in Houston, TX, and open to relocating for the right opportunity. Right now I'm looking for my next role as an **AI Engineer, Generative AI Engineer, Software Engineer, or Data Scientist** — somewhere I can keep building systems that are reliable enough to trust and interesting enough to keep learning from.

When I'm not working, I'm usually prototyping a side project that runs on a schedule and tells me when it breaks (see MarketPulse AI below 👇).
